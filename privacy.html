Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SafeTalk ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the SafeTalk application and website (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text), an optional profile avatar, and account settings such as role and status.
  • Recovery information: a hashed recovery code used to regain access to your account without email.
  • Conversation content: the messages you send and receive in the Service, stored so the conversation can be shown to you.
  • Usage and security data: basic usage limits and audit records (for example, administrative actions) used to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service, including displaying your conversations and generating supportive replies.
  • To authenticate you, verify your email, and let you reset or recover your account.
  • To keep the Service safe, including moderation and abuse prevention.
  • To send you service-related emails such as email verification and password reset messages.

3. AI processing

To generate supportive replies, a limited portion of your recent conversation (by default, the most recent twelve messages) is sent to our third-party AI provider, Google (Gemini API), for processing. We do not use your conversations to train our own models. How the provider handles data is governed by that provider's terms and policies.

4. Google user data (Gmail API)

SafeTalk uses the Google Gmail API with the gmail.send scope only. This lets SafeTalk send emails on behalf of the Gmail account owner, such as account verification and password reset messages.

  • We do not read, scan, store, or share the content of your Gmail inbox or messages.
  • We use the gmail.send scope solely to send service-related emails that you or the account owner trigger.
  • OAuth credentials (including any refresh token) are stored securely on our servers and are not shared with third parties except as required to send email through Google's API.
  • You can revoke SafeTalk's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

SafeTalk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data retention

  • Messages are retained for 90 days by default and then automatically deleted.
  • Messages that have been flagged (for example, for safety review) may be retained longer.
  • Audit records are retained for 90 days by default.
  • When you delete your account, your account data and associated conversations are deleted, subject to any records we must keep for legal or security reasons.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service (for example, cloud hosting, database, AI processing, and email delivery), and where required by law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including hashing passwords, encrypted connections, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can review and update your account settings in the Service.
  • You can delete your account, which removes your data as described above.
  • You can revoke Gmail API access at any time via your Google Account permissions.
  • To make a request about your information, contact us using the address below.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under the age required by your local law to consent to data processing, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date.

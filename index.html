What SafeTalk is

SafeTalk is a chat application designed to feel calm and unhurried. It offers supportive, non-clinical conversation and a simple interface that avoids pressure, streaks, or scoring. You can talk at your own pace and leave whenever you want.

Features

  • Simple, low-pressure chat interface
  • AI-assisted supportive replies
  • Private conversations tied to your account
  • Account controls including data deletion
SafeTalk is not a medical or crisis service. It does not provide diagnosis, therapy, or emergency support. If you are in crisis or may be in danger, contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately.
